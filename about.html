About Us

The story behind Music & Movie Trivia — The Game

Who We Are

Music & Movie Trivia is developed and operated by HDMultiMedia, a small independent software studio based in Florida, United States. We build fun, free, family-friendly web experiences — no subscriptions, no paywalls, no nonsense.

Our team is passionate about music, movies, and the joy of friendly competition. We created this game because we believe trivia nights should be accessible to everyone, whether you're playing solo at midnight or hosting a game night for ten.

What We Built

Music & Movie Trivia offers 4,800 unique questions across 24 hand-crafted categories — 12 Music and 12 Movie. Questions span Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulty levels, covering everything from Classic Hollywood to Modern Music, from Animated Classics to Grammy Glory.

The game runs entirely in your browser with no app download required, works on mobile and desktop, and supports up to 10 players in hot-seat MultiPlayer mode — all on a single device.

Our Mission

We believe entertainment should be free. Music & Movie Trivia is and always will be 100% free to play. We keep the lights on through Google AdSense advertising — nothing more.

We also take privacy seriously. We collect the absolute minimum information needed to run the leaderboard, never sell your data, and give you full control to delete your account at any time.
