What Is Music & Movie Trivia?

Music & Movie Trivia is a free online trivia game packed with 4,800 questions spanning 24 categories — 12 Music and 12 Movie. Whether you're a solo player chasing a spot on the Worldwide Leaderboard or hosting a family game night with up to 10 players on one device, there's something here for everyone.

No download required, no subscription, no sign-up needed to play as a guest. Just open the site and start playing — it's that simple.

How to Play

  • Single Player: Create a free account to compete on the Worldwide Leaderboard and track your cumulative score across every session you play.
  • MultiPlayer: Add 2 to 10 players on one device and take turns answering questions in hot-seat style — perfect for family game nights, parties, and get-togethers.
  • Guest Play: Jump straight in with no account required. Play anonymously with zero sign-up friction.

Each question is timed — answer faster to score more points. Difficulty varies by category, and each correct answer is revealed with a satisfying explanation so you actually learn something while you play.
