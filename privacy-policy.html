Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 1, 2026  |  Operated by: HDMultiMedia, Florida, United States

Hey, We Keep It Simple

At Music & Movie Trivia, your privacy matters to us. We built this game to be fun, free, and trustworthy. This policy explains clearly — in plain English — exactly what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it. No confusing legal jargon. Promise.

What Information We Collect

We collect as little as possible:

  • Single Player accounts: Your first and last name (or chosen display name) and email address. That's it. We use your email only to identify your account so your leaderboard scores are saved across visits.
  • MultiPlayer games: First names only for each player — used only to display scores during the game session. These names are permanently deleted the moment you quit the MultiPlayer game.
  • Guests: We collect absolutely nothing. No name, no email, no data of any kind.

Why We Collect It

  • Your name is displayed on the Worldwide Leaderboard so other players can see your ranking.
  • Your email is used solely to identify your returning account and keep your cumulative scores accurate.
  • We do not use your email for marketing, newsletters, or promotions — ever.

How We Store and Protect Your Data

Your account data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption and security practices. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Your email address is never displayed publicly — only your display name appears on the leaderboard.

Google AdSense & Advertising

Music & Movie Trivia is free to play and supported by Google AdSense advertising. Google may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. For more information on how Google uses data from sites that use their services, visit Google's Privacy & Terms.

Cookies

Our site uses cookies primarily through Google AdSense to deliver relevant advertisements. We do not use cookies to collect personal information about you beyond what is described in this policy. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties — period. The only exception is Google AdSense, which operates under its own privacy policy to serve ads on our site.

Your Rights — Deleting Your Data

You are in full control of your data:

  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time directly from within the game by clicking Quit Game → Yes, Delete Everything.
  • This permanently removes your name, email, and all scores from our system immediately.
  • You may also contact us directly to request data deletion.

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Music & Movie Trivia is designed for general audiences and is safe for family use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information without your consent, please contact us immediately at Privacy@MusicMovieTrivia.com and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the game after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
